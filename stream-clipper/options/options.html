StreamClip Pro settings

Everything here applies to newly started captures.

Rolling buffer

While a stream is monitored, StreamClip keeps the most recent footage in memory so you can clip backwards. Nothing is written to disk until you clip.

Clipping

Capture mode

Source buffers the broadcaster's own video segments — the exact bytes the CDN sends, with no decoding or re-encoding, so clips match the broadcast quality. It also grabs the stream's best rendition regardless of the quality you're watching at, so you can watch in 720p to save bandwidth and still clip in 1080p60. It needs the site to use HLS (Twitch and Kick always do). Tab records what the tab renders — it works on every site, but re-encodes.

Bandwidth: source mode downloads the stream a second time, in parallel with the player, so it roughly doubles your usage — and buffering a rendition higher than the one you're watching costs more still. If your connection is tight and the stream stutters while monitoring, lower the cap below or switch to tab mode.

Tab-capture quality

These apply only when recording rendered video (tab mode). Source mode ignores them — it never re-encodes, and always captures the video alone. In tab mode, quality can't exceed what the player shows, so set the stream to its highest quality first.
